Cart lists line items with quantity steppers and a sticky order summary with a gold Proceed to Checkout button, plus the controls a delivery customer expects.

Provide `items` (`id`, `name`, `price`, `qty`, optional `detail`, `image`, `stock`), plus `deliveryFee` and `discount`. Quantity is clamped between 1 and `stock`. Each line has Save for later (`onWishlist(item)`) and Remove; Clear cart needs a second tap; Continue shopping calls `onContinue`. A progress bar shows the spend left for free delivery (`freeDeliveryFrom`). A pincode check sets the zone fee (`checkDelivery(pincode)` returns `{serviceable, zone, fee, eta, message}`; the built-in rule is a demo, wire it to your delivery zones). Promo codes come from `promoCodes` (`{CODE: {type: 'percent' | 'flat', value, min}}`) or an async `onApplyPromo(code, subtotal)` for server validation. Rider tip options default to 0, 10, 20, 30 (`tips={false}` hides them) and delivery instructions take up to 200 characters. `onCheckout(total, {note, tip, promo, pincode, zone})` starts checkout. The totals are a preview: the server recomputes prices, promo, fee and stock, and the cart is cleared only after payment is confirmed, never on this button.
